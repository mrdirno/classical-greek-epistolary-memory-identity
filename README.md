## The Story

A freed debtor must reconstruct his past from the fragments of other men's lives, forging truth from memory in the law courts of Athens.

## Entry Points for Contributors

**Start anywhere. Fork freely. No permission needed.**

### Story Entry Points

- Write a 'lost' letter from Kleon's father, Nikias, written on the eve of the doomed shipping venture, revealing his fears and hopes.
- Compose the internal monologue of Lysias as he assesses Kleon for the first time in the prison courtyard. What does he see, and what past of his own does it echo?
- Draft the speech Kleon writes for a client accused of a crime he did commit, exploring how to craft persuasive truth from immoral facts.
- Create an entry from the prison gaoler's log, detailing the arrival, condition, and departure of prisoner 'Athenian Debtor #7'.

### Technical Entry Points

- Build an interactive 'Memory Palace' website where users can navigate the fragments of Kleon's past (letters, legal depositions, sensory memories) to reconstruct the narrative.
- Develop a text-generation tool that mimics a 'logographer's workshop,' where a user inputs case facts and the AI generates persuasive Athenian-style rhetorical arguments in different voices (Lysias's precision, Kleon's rawness).
